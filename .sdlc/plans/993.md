## Behaviour
- `context.documents` (list of repo-relative strings) adds documents after the two built-in ones.
  Absent or wrong-typed: nothing changes, output byte-identical.
- Each entry is `present`, `absent` or `unreachable`. A path that is absolute, contains a parent
  step that leaves the repo root, or resolves through a link outside the root is unreachable with a reason and is never read.
- `context.inline_max_bytes` (default 16384; positive whole number, booleans rejected): a present
  document above it renders as a pointer line, not a body.
- An explicit empty list adds a gap line saying no documents were named. Absent key adds none.
- Absent or unreachable configured documents add a gap line each (stated, never silent).
