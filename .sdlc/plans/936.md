## Behaviour
- classify(pre, call, pr, parents) is pure. merged: PR merged, merge commit has two parents, second is the
  verified head, first is the base seen before the call. merged-with-warning: second parent is the head but the
  first is not the base (base moved) or the parent count is not two. refused: the call errored and a read shows
  the PR open and unmerged. unconfirmed: lost ack or timeout, failed read, no pre-merge observation, parents
  unread, or a merge of some other head. armed only for an unmerged open PR with a non-null auto_merge.
- Pending record at state/unit-landings/<unit-key>.json, key folded after the unit-name guard (record_path).
  begin writes it BEFORE the merge call; finish rewrites with the outcome and deletes on merged; settle does one
  PR read and one commit read through injected readers; prune ages out stale records.
- run_landing orders begin, call, read-back, classify, finish; a failed begin means the call is never made.
- Gate: every writing or reporting entry point asks feature_upkeep.enabled; closed means no file, no row.
- doctor_row reads only; reports the oldest pending age and the re-run gesture.
- Pruning: goal_state_prune.FILE_FAMILIES is keyed by terminal goals and cannot own a unit-keyed store, so the
  module owns an age prune (as the resolution store does) and the disposition names it.
