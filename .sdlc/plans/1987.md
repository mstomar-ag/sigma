# Plan #1987: pages get titles without a hand-made titles file

## Files
- new `skills/sigma-loop/scripts/dag_titles.py`: `run(sdlc, now=None, run=None)` lists open issues with `gh_api.list_issues` (REST, 100 per page, cap `TITLES_LIST_CAP` = 5000 so at most 50 calls), keeps earlier-file titles for map members not in the listing (closed members), scrubs each with `dag_html.clean_title` (same cut), writes `<sdlc>/state/dag-titles.json` atomically: `{"schema": "dag-titles/1", "read_at": <iso>, "titles": {"<n>": "<title>"}}`. - tests: `tests/test_dag_titles_1987.py`.

## Behaviour and size
Calls = ceil(open/100): 10 calls at 1,000 open (10x), capped at 50 at 5,000+ (100x; the rest is left out and the line says so). Titles for closed members cost no call.
