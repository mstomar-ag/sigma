# Plan 1138: draft a rule from a learned pattern, with a held-back exam

## Files
new module (small, library plus main) next to rulebook.py/autonomy.py, wiring a verb into the existing propose flow, tests, registry/inventory entries the new writer needs.

## Behaviour
- `list` verb: question kind and area pairs at autonomy level >= 2 with agreement >= a configurable threshold (default documented), from the decision store.
- `draft` verb: builds a proposed rule from the majority option, linking the source record ids; hands it to the existing rulebook propose flow (status proposed). Approval stays with the owner through the existing approve flow.
- Held-back exam: split the pair's decisions deterministically; draft on one part, test on the held-out part; fail closed (no draft) when held-out set too small or agreement below threshold.
- Gated by the existing rulebook/autonomy gates; closed means inert.
- Repository-owned: no person field.

## Test list
- list respects level and threshold; draft links source ids; held-out exam passes and fails closed on small/disagreeing sets; draft goes through propose (never approved); gate closed inert; verb help registered in script-help and write-surface tests.

## Risks
Context-budget ratchet (no SKILL.md growth), registries for new writers, test_script_help. Smallest design: one module, two subcommands.
