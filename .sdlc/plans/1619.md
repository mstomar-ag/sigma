## Behaviour notes
- Config key `discovery.dag.mode`; `dag_store.mode(config)` is the only reader. A typo reads shadow.
- Default (shadow) with no stored map: map side answers "unusable", nothing logged (nothing compared), old decision returned.
- `.sdlc` unwritable: logging fails silently.
