# Plan #1505: duplicate search must not cross units

Goal: a follow-up filed from a goal of unit X is never folded into an issue of unit Y; the Y match is mentioned as related.

## Files
- skills/sigma-loop/scripts/handoff.py: after `_duplicate_search` in the filing path, drop "duplicate" strength candidates whose unit differs from the filing unit (`unit`, inherited/targeted). Unit of a candidate via `source.fetch_body_labels(ref)` + `features.read` (same helpers `feature_stamp.unit_of` uses). Same unit, or either side with no unit and no source surface (local mode): unchanged. Cross-unit matches stay in `candidates` as related and are listed in the new issue body ("Related: #N (other unit)").
- CHANGELOG.md: one line under Unreleased.
- tests/test_handoff_dedup_unit.py (new).

- Never raises.
