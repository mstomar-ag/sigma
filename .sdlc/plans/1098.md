## Behaviour
- `valid_ref` rejects non-strings, empty, a leading dash or slash, whitespace/control, `~^:?*[\`, `..`, `@{`, `//`,
  trailing slash/dot, a component starting with a dot or ending `.lock`.
- The job's engine child (already behind the project and machine doors) calls the entry point after the legacy pass and adds
  the result word under `unit_pass` on its last output line. The entry point is itself gated (project door). No base
  configured: nothing is called. An exception reads `failed`; the line is still printed.
- Gate closed: `run_engine` answers closed before the body; nothing is called and nothing is written.
- The legacy `outcome`/`unit_tip` fields are untouched, so the job record and notes are unchanged.

## Risks
- Double work: the legacy pass and the entry point can both rewrite a behind unit in one run. The second sees a unit
  that is current (rebase is a no-op, the lease push carries the same tip). Not measured at scale; a follow-up should pick
  one path.
- Refs are the remote-tracking ones; the legacy pass fetches first, the entry point does not, so a stale ref reads
  not-landed or unreadable, both harmless.
- Rebase onto a clean tree only; conflicts abort and read `failed`, nothing is resolved.
