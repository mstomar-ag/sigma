# Plan 1624: generated DAG.md and the all-writers history reader

Files: new `skills/sigma-loop/scripts/dag_render.py` (pure render of merged records), `dag_history.py`
(`read_all(root)`: every `dag/YYYY-MM/*.jsonl` under a ledger worktree, corrupt lines skipped; writer count and
newest record age), `dag.py` (`history --all`), `sync.py` (`_write_dag(sdlc, path)` next to `_write_team`; called in
`publish` and in `regenerate_team`; stage `DAG.md`; only when ledger on and mode not off), `doctor.py` (row
`dag ledger: N writers, newest record <age>`, absent when ledger off or mode off or no files), write-surface
inventory entry, CHANGELOG under Unreleased.

Behaviour: render folds records sorted by (t, ev, a, b, n, chain): the latest edge-added/edge-removed per (a,b)
wins; node-resolved/node-deleted mark nodes; chains = weak components of present edges. Sections: Stuck chains
(unresolved root blockers, most waiting nodes first, ties by number), Active chains, Cycles, Completed this month
(chain-completed records in the newest record's month), Counts. Numbers only, `#N` form. Same record set in any
order gives identical bytes. PROVISIONAL (owner to ratify): history carries no parked state, so "stuck" means an
unresolved node that holds other unresolved nodes and has no unresolved blocker of its own.
Regeneration after a rebase conflict: DAG.md resolves to theirs (`-X theirs`) then `regenerate_team` rewrites it
from the merged files and amends. Ledger off or no worktree: nothing generated.
