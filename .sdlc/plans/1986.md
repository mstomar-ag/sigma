# Plan #1986: the page of a goal also draws the work that waits on it

## Files
- `skills/sigma-loop/scripts/dag_html.py`: `build` adds the open dependents; `render_page`/`_header`/CSS/legend draw them.
- `tests/test_dag_html.py` (or a new `tests/test_dag_dependents_1986.py`): the tests.
- `docs/dag.md`, `skills/sigma-dag/SKILL.md` (offset any added context-budget words), CHANGELOG `## Unreleased`.

## Behaviour
- `DEPENDENT_CAP = 40` (module constant, stated in the docstring). In `build`, after the plan members: walk `g.dependents` from the goal breadth-first over nodes that are open issues (`state == "open"`, kind issue), skipping nodes already drawn; nearest first, ties by number; stop at the cap. `left_out` = open dependents not drawn; `waiting` = all open transitive dependents.
- Dependent node: `m="waiting"`, class `dependent` (muted: reduced opacity, own colour), `why="waits on #N, directly or through another issue"`, `dist` = steps from the goal; column = goal column + dist. Edges among drawn nodes where the blocker is the goal or a dependent are tagged `holds`, drawn with their own class.
- The prerequisite half is untouched: `open`, `depth`, `decisions`, `who`, `stops`, forecast count only plan members (dependents are excluded from `open_rows`), so the page still matches `chain.map_closure`.
- Heading facts: `<b>K</b> open issues wait on it`; when `left_out`, a notice: "N more open issues wait on #G and are not drawn (cap 40)". A leaf (K=0) draws no fact and no dependents.
- Legend lists the `waiting` state when present. Plan pages (`run_plan`) unchanged.
- Size: dependents add at most 40 nodes and 40+ edges per page (+~25 KB); at 10x (400) and 100x (4,000) dependents the page draws the same 40 and writes one count; cost of the walk is O(open dependents + their edges).
