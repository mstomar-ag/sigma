## Done when
- [ ] `predict_effort` applies the same rules as the tier axis for `high`: a title hit wins on one occurrence; a body-only match needs two or more occurrences; a match inside a code span or a path-shaped token never counts. The `low` axis keeps its own rules unless the design says otherwise.
- [ ] `predict.py why` reports the effort signal, its location and its hit count, as it does for the tier.
- [ ] Tests: a single incidental body word stays medium; two body hits or one title hit is high; a code-span hit never counts. Each goes red when its clause is removed on its own.
- [ ] The CHANGELOG `## Unreleased` entry states the measured share above and that live spend after the change is not measured.
